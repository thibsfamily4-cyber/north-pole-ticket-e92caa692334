🧝 How It All Hooks Up

The elves drew the plan. Five cables cross the floor, and that's it.

  1. 🖥️Big screen: one fiber HDMI cable runs from Dad's PC to the G9.
  2. 🏎️Wheel: the pedals and H-shifter plug into the wheel base, and the base gets its own USB extension to the PC.
  3. 🔗Hub: the Sabrent hub on the rig holds the handbrake and the shaker's sound card, with a second extension to the PC.
  4. 🔊Sound: the Arena 9 subwoofer sits up front on a third extension. The rear speakers are wireless.
  5. ⚡Power: one cord feeds the power strip mounted on the rig. Everything plugs in there.
  6. 🎀Tidy: cables hide under the floor covers and get sleeved up the stand and rig. Zero spaghetti.
